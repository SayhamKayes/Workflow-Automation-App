Privacy Policy for LogCrafter

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Information We Access

LogCrafter connects with your Google Account only after your explicit permission to create and manage your personal task and workflow log spreadsheets in your Google Drive.

2. How We Use Information

We access your email address and profile name to personalize your dashboard session. All task entries and workflow hours you record are saved directly into your own personal Google Sheets spreadsheet. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your spreadsheet data remains entirely within your Google Drive account under your control. LogCrafter does not store your private workflow entries on external servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer at sayhamkayes101@gmail.com.
